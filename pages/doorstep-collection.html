SERVICE OVERVIEW

Concierge-level convenience for your residents

Our doorstep collection service transforms waste management from a chore into a premium amenity. Uniformed team members collect waste directly from residents' doors on scheduled days, eliminating trips to dumpsters and enhancing the living experience at your property.

This service is particularly valuable for luxury properties, senior communities, and any multi-family development where resident convenience and property differentiation are priorities.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple, reliable process for consistent results

1

Resident Preparation

Residents place waste bags outside their doors by the designated time (typically evening before pickup day). We provide clear guidelines and optional door hangers for reminders.

2

Professional Collection

Our uniformed team arrives during scheduled hours, quietly collecting waste from each door. We use efficient routes and maintain a respectful, low-impact presence.

3

Proper Disposal

Collected waste is transported to designated disposal areas or off-site facilities, ensuring compliance with all municipal regulations and property requirements.

4

Service Documentation

We provide detailed service reports including pickup completion times, any issues encountered, and recommendations for service optimization.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about doorstep collection

We collect standard household waste, recycling, and small bags. Items must be properly bagged and not exceed weight limits. Hazardous materials, large items, and liquids require special handling through our bulk waste service.
All team members undergo background checks and are uniformed for easy identification. We work with property management to establish secure access protocols and can provide service during specific hours to minimize security concerns.
We maintain consistent pickup schedules and provide advance notice of any changes. If waste is placed out late, we can often accommodate on a case-by-case basis. We also offer resident education to improve participation rates.
Pricing is based on property size, pickup frequency, and service level. We offer transparent, flat-rate pricing with no hidden fees. Most properties see cost savings compared to traditional waste services when factoring in reduced maintenance and improved resident satisfaction.
