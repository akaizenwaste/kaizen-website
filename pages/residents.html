RESIDENT BENEFITS

Make waste management easy and convenient

At Kaizen Waste, we believe that waste management should be simple and convenient. Our doorstep collection service and smart waste solutions make it easy to maintain a clean and comfortable living environment.

No more trips to the dumpster, no more worrying about timing - just place your waste outside your door and we'll take care of the rest. Our reliable service ensures your home stays clean while contributing to a better community environment.

Doorstep Collection

No more trips to the dumpster - we collect your waste right from your doorstep at the same time every night.

Consistent Service

Reliable nightly pickup at the same time, every time - you can set your watch to it.

Proper Waste Sorting

Our runners sort your waste and recycling at the dumpsters - ensuring proper handling and disposal.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple steps for hassle-free waste management

1

Bag Your Waste

Bag and tie your waste in the community provided Kaizen Can. No special bags required - use your regular trash bags.

2

Place Outside Your Door

Place the Kaizen Can outside your door at the designated time. Our team will collect it during their nightly rounds.

3

We Handle the Rest

Our runners take care of everything - from collection to proper sorting and disposal at the community dumpsters.
