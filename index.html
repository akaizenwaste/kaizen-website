FAQ

Which services do you handle?

Kaizen manages doorstep collection, bulk & on-demand removal, enclosure resets, and waste advisory for multifamily communities. Tell us what you need and we’ll build the nightly playbook.

How fast can you launch at a new community?

Most properties go live within 10-14 days. We complete a site assessment, deliver resident communications, and train crews before the first pickup so launch night is seamless.

How is pricing structured?

We structure pricing per door per month with volume tiers for portfolios. Bulk and advisory services are scoped à la carte so you only pay for the coverage you need.

How do residents get support during service?

Residents can text, email, or scan a QR code to reach the WasteOS support desk. Our team responds within minutes and loops your onsite staff in on every resolution.
