The farm of the future

Farms today look a lot like they did 20 years ago.

The farm of the future will look nothing like the farm of today. The farm of the future will be run by AI – taking in data about every plant, all the time, and autonomously making the best decisions that lead to producing the best crop.

We believe the farm of the future will be fully automated, but getting there is not a straight shot.
You can't automate anything without first knowing where the inefficiencies are.

And you can't solve for inefficiency without a way to put that data into practice.

Here at Orchard, we are building the groundwork of systems, data, and software that will enable the farm of the future to become exactly as we envision.

Our data is the first step towards the farm of the future.

FAQ's

What crops do you support?

Currently, we serve apple, grape, blueberry, and cherry farms. We are growing rapidly and adding new crops that we support every season. If you don't see what you farm, check back soon!

What is the turnaround time?

We understand data is the bedrock of farm decision-making. Data coming in two days too late could very well mean that a critical pruning, spraying, or thinning decision is not made on time. Our FruitScope Vision Systems process data on-device, overnight, giving our growers a typical turnaround time of the very next morning. The post-processed data uploads to FruitScope OS for you to immediately begin making decisions based on your data.

How much does it cost?

Access to our technology is priced on a per-acre, per-year basis. We've priced our system to be competitive, while fitting the needs of our growers. We offer large-acreage discounts for large farms. If you are interested in learning more about our pricing, or setting up a live demo of our tech at your farm, please contact us and reach out below!

How are farms using your technology today?

Growers, warehouses, sales desks, and farm management companies throughout the U.S. and worldwide are using our technology today to automate their farm management. Our data and technology provides value throughout the entirety of a farm's operation.
